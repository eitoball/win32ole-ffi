# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial release: WIN32OLE and WIN32OLE::Type, TypeLib, Method, Param,
  Variable, Variant, Record and Event in pure Ruby with Fiddle, tested on
  Windows with CRuby 2.6+ (mswin/mingw) and JRuby 9.4+.
- The deprecated toplevel names (WIN32OLE_TYPE, WIN32OLERuntimeError, ...)
  as aliases, as in ruby/win32ole.
- RBS signatures under `sig/`.
